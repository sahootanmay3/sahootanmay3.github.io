Research

Statistics for lifetime, reliability, and constrained structure

My research develops probabilistic and inferential tools for systems and lifetime data in which censoring, dependence, contamination, or qualitative order restrictions are scientifically meaningful.

01

Survival Analysis

Censored-data inference, semiparametric lifetime models, transformation regression, cure and frailty structures, and flexible computation.

02

Reliability Theory

Coherent systems, random weighted systems, redundancy allocation, component importance, dependence, ageing, and survival capacity.

03

Robust Inference

Divergence-based estimation, generalized likelihood, robust testing, and procedures stable under contamination or misspecification.

04

Stochastic Orders

Stochastic comparison, order-restricted inference, mixtures, sequential order statistics, and dependence governed by copulas.

Current directions

Methodological programme

Robust inference with censoring

Developing robust estimation and testing procedures for censored survival data, including restricted-time formulations and divergence-based methods.

Structured survival regression

Studying flexible survival regression models while preserving interpretable lifetime functionals, transformation structure, and scientifically motivated constraints.

Stochastic-order-constrained inference

Constructing tests and estimators for hazard, cumulative-hazard, and mean-residual-life ordering using convex minorants, concave majorants, and shape restrictions.

Reliability

Systems and dependence

Random weighted coherent systems

Investigating structure-based performance measures, component allocation, and system comparison when components contribute through random weights.

Mixtures and heterogeneous populations

Establishing stochastic comparison and ageing results for finite mixtures and heterogeneous subpopulations.

Order statistics and copulas

Studying multivariate comparison, dependence, and ageing properties of ordered random vectors and sequential order statistics.
