Research programme

Inference for complex lifetime data

My work combines probability, statistical inference, and reliability theory to study ageing, dependence, censoring, robustness, and constrained structure.

01

Survival Analysis

Methods for censored lifetime data, transformation models, cure and frailty structures, and modern computational approaches.

02

Reliability Theory

Coherent systems, redundancy allocation, component importance, ageing, and random weighted system structures.

03

Robust Inference

Divergence-based estimation and testing methods designed to retain stability under contamination and model misspecification.

04

Stochastic Orders

Order-restricted inference and stochastic comparison of mixtures, systems, order statistics, and dependent lifetime models.
