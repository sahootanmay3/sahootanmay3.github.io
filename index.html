Tanmay Sahoo

Assistant Professor, Department of Mathematics
Indian Institute of Technology Palakkad

After completing my undergraduate and postgraduate studies from Vidyasagar University, I obtained a Ph.D. in Probability and Statistics from the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, India, under the supervision of Dr. Nil Kamal Hazra. Subsequently, I was a postdoc in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, India, under the supervision of Prof. Neeraj Misra. I work primarily in the areas of statistics and reliability theory.

Research

Research interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of Survival Analysis, Reliability Theory, Stochastic Order-Restricted Inference, and Robust Statistical Inference.

Survival Analysis

Statistical modelling and inference for censored lifetime data, including cure, frailty, competing-risk, transformation, and mean-residual-life models.

Explore this

Reliability Theory & Stochastic Models

Stochastic modelling and inference for coherent systems, sequential order statistics, load-sharing structures, ageing, and component allocation.

Explore this

Robust Statistical Inference

Divergence-, generalized-likelihood-, and spacing-based procedures designed to retain inferential stability under contamination, censoring, and model misspecification.

Explore this

Order-Restricted Inference

Nonparametric and semiparametric estimation and testing under stochastic-order, hazard, ageing, and shape restrictions.
