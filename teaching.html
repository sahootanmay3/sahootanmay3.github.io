Teaching approach

Theory with computation

My teaching connects mathematical arguments with simulation and reproducible statistical computing. Course materials are designed to help students move between formal inference, numerical experiments, and practical interpretation.

  • Simulation for understanding statistical theory
  • Reproducible statistical computing in R
  • Functional and survival-data applications
  • Advanced inference under model constraints
